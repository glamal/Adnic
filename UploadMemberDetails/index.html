Product Guidelines

  • Upload members with format [*Relation, *Gender, *DOB, *Salary Type, *Visa Location, *Category, *Marital Status].
  • A group with minimum 26 employees and with minimum 50 members (Employees + Dependents), can now choose 5 categories under enhanced plan + 1 EBP category.
  • Maximum 5 Enhanced + 1 EBP Categories allowed.
  • Minimum 11 members in a group with 5 employees.
  • Minimum 5 employees upto a maximum of 150 members.
  • Excel Sheet Name should be 'Sheet1'.
  • Members with age 65 and above will be subject to Medical Underwriting.
  • Date Of Birth Format Should be ('DD-MMM-YYYY' [Eg: 01-Jan-2000] or 'DD-MMM-YY' [Eg: 01-Jan-00] or 'D-MMM-YYYY' [Eg: 1-Jan-2000] or 'D-MMM-YY' [Eg: 1-Jan-00]).
